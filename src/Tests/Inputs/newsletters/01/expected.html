Happy holidays from our family to yours!

 

Holiday newsletters are a great way to keep the people closest to you updated on your family's life. It's also a fun way to share holiday greetings or write about your past year's experiences. To start, let each of your family write down their own articles in the newsletter. Add photographs from family vacations. Think about what you would tell your family and friends if you could see them. 

A family newsletter is also a fun activity to do as a family. Everyone feels included when you share memories with each other. Sit down and talk about the important things that happened in the past year. Also, think about who's reading your newsletter. What would your friends or distant relatives most want to hear about? Write down your ideas and then organize your list into categories.

Adding pictures to your newsletter

 

Pictures of your family trips or special events can bring your newsletter to life. Once you've chosen a picture, place it close to the information. Be sure to put the caption of the image near the image. Want to use online images in your newsletter? Find thousands of images online to download and insert into your newsletter. 

To change the pictures in this newsletter to your own, select the image you wish to change. Go to Insert, Pictures, and then select either Pictures or Online Pictures. Locate the image you want and double-click it. The new picture will add to the existing text box for easy positioning.

Tip: Borders may not show on text boxes that contain images or text. Before you add anything else, make sure it's placed within a text box.
