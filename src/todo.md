# Rendering fidelity todo

Deep comparison of every scenario in `src/Tests/Inputs/` (385 fixtures: 319 Word with 555 reference pages, 40 Excel, 26 PowerPoint): `expected_*.png` (Word, 150 DPI, via RenderHelper) versus `skia_result#page_*.verified.png`, `imagesharp_result#page_*.verified.png`, `pdf_result#page_*.verified.png` (PDFium render), and `html_result.verified.png` (headless-browser screenshot of the HTML export).

Each finding: `severity | backends | pages | description`. `all` = skia+imagesharp+pdf. HTML findings ignore pagination/viewport-width reflow by design and only flag content/styling errors. Not reported: anti-aliasing texture, 1-2px subpixel shifts, ImageSharp's softer glyph rasterization. `[known]` = already documented as accepted in that scenario's notes.md. How a difference is judged — crops versus metrics, and what counts as noise — is `docs/fidelity-audit.md`.

**This file lists only what is still wrong.** A finding is deleted the moment it lands, and its durable knowledge moves to `docs/word-features.md` (feature behaviour and the evidence behind it), `docs/floating-art-pipeline.md` (anchored/floating art), `docs/html-import.md` (HTML and AltChunk input), `src/page_counts.md` (page-count experiment ledger) or `docs/fidelity-audit.md` (comparison method). Nothing that has shipped is described here — for how a fix was reached, read those docs and the git history. This is a temporary working document; it is expected to shrink to nothing.

**Open: 0 major, 0 medium, 1 minor = 1 findings across 1 scenarios.** The tally is recounted from the `severity | backends | pages` lines below rather than hand-adjusted (hand-adjusted, it drifted twice). Not every finding has been re-read against the current baselines: every MAJOR was re-judged against Word's page on 2026-10-01; the findings on the 105 fixtures whose Word references were regenerated in Calibri on 2026-10-02 were measured against the Aptos references they replaced and have not been re-read since; and a finding that names a specific block N px off is usually still live where one describing whole-page drift is usually stale (aligning each page's ink profile against Word's gives a median page offset of 2px, but a median worst local displacement of 11px). How a landing moved the corpus is in its commit message and in `src/page_counts.md`, not here.

## Systemic issues (cross-scenario root causes)

These patterns repeat across many scenarios; fixing one clears whole families of the per-scenario findings below. **IDs are stable** — findings reference them by number — so a closed issue's number is retired rather than reused, and the list is deliberately gappy.

### All raster + PDF backends

- **#25 Word's row-split trigger** — landed 2026-08-07 with the strict floor fit, the vMerge tie rule and style-inherited `w:pageBreakBefore`, and whole-table floor strictness on 2026-09-05 (`docs/word-features.md`, Multi-page Tables / Page Break Before; the attempts and their measurements are in the git history). Two of the LibreOffice leads were probed on 2026-11-01 (`_probe_rowsplit`) and neither held: Word does not charge a split row's floor across fragments, it moves a row floored past a page whole and cuts it at the page's height; and the fragment that ends a split row shares its closing rule with the next row (`docs/word-features.md`, Multi-page Tables). A third, `COLLAPSE_EMPTY_CELL_PARA`, was probed the same day (`_probe_emptycell`) and Word collapses no empty cell, as Morph already did not (`docs/word-features.md`, Multi-page Tables). Still open, un-probed: the broader always-on flag inventory (`sw/inc/IDocumentSettingAccess.hxx:36-106`, DOCX defaults `WriterFilter.cxx:300-341`) — `HEADER_SPACING_BELOW_LAST_PARA`, `TREAT_SINGLE_COLUMN_BREAK_AS_PAGE_BREAK` and `IGNORE_TABS_AND_BLANKS_FOR_LINE_CALCULATION`.
- **#43 Text advances: the breaking and autofit rules are settled; what is still open is listed here.** Word breaks lines and sizes autofit columns on the font's plain linear advances at the size as authored, with GPOS kerning scaled the same way (Word-probed 2026-10-02: `docs/layout-engine.md`, "The crux"; `docs/word-features.md`, Table Auto-fit and Kerning), and every face has measured that way since. The per-glyph `.wordadvances` sidecars and the space-compression wedge that stood in for it from 2026-08-30 are gone, with no page count moved (`src/page_counts.md`, experiment 23). Kerning followed the same day: `w:kern` comes down the style ladder as Word takes it, and the measurer adds it linearly and across spaces (`docs/word-features.md`, Kerning). Still open, each measured:
  - **An autofit column takes its text's unrounded linear width, and Word's runs a fraction of a pixel over that**: 0.3px in compatibility mode 12 and 0.7px in mode 15, behind a hairline border (990 probed tables). Unmodelled; it is under a pixel and moves no break.
  - **Glyph placement inside a line** landed 2026-10-31 as whole-pixel DRAWING widths (`CanonicalParagraphMeasurer.DrawAtWordPixels`, `docs/word-features.md`, Kerning): each run is drawn as wide as Word's pen takes it, glyphs spread evenly across it. Still open: each glyph on its own pixel rather than spread across its run, the glyph RENDER size em-rounded, Word's 1/16px kern snapping, and lines with tabs, leaders or inline pictures, which are drawn as laid out. The render size is settled and was tried on 2026-11-01: Word's XPS declares unrotated text at a whole number of 120-dpi pixels across every probe document (11pt at 10.8, 10pt at 10.2, 8pt at 7.8; 9 and 12pt as written). Drawing `DrawAtWordPixels`' runs at that size, with the spread widened by the width the smaller glyphs gave up, scored 310 pages worse and 200 better (SSIM −1.22 in all) and was reverted: labels/15's address lines ended 2px short of Word's, so the spread does not follow the advances the painters draw with, and has to be worked out from those before the size can land.
  - Text set in a face that is not bundled is measured on a substitute's metrics: business-plans/15's Univers falls back to Tahoma (measured closest at −5.9% against the real Univers installed on the reference machine), and Segoe UI Semibold, Franklin Gothic Demi and Avenir Next LT Pro Light are not bundled either. Their wraps are approximations; the honest fix for Univers is a licensed Univers-metric face, which is out of Morph's hands. They are proprietary, which `src/Fonts/README.md` rules out bundling; the open-licensed ones the cache holds went in on 2026-10-11 (Karla, Source Sans Pro, Playfair Display and Work Sans faces) and closed letters/09, letters/10, business-plans/04 and business-plans/06.
  - The suite's confirmation pass has not always been deterministic on near-fit scenarios: on 2026-09-06 `regenerate-baselines.sh` promoted resumes/16 from its first pass and the second pass rendered both Skia and ImageSharp pages differently on five consecutive regenerations, as did a third Skia-only run, and business-plans/15 did the same on 2026-09-05. Host renders of the same input are byte-identical across four processes, so the variance lives in the parallel test process, not in the layout rules. Re-examined 2026-09-24 and NOT reproduced: every Word scenario rendered on Skia and ImageSharp in two separate host processes (12-way parallel, shuffled order) hashed byte-identical, as did a sequential pass against three in-process parallel rounds; the measurer's caches are per-conversion and nothing in the engine shares mutable state across conversions. The 2026-09-24 and 2026-10-02 regenerations both confirmed clean, the second reproducing an earlier run of the same code byte for byte across 782 files. `regenerate-baselines.sh` restores any promoted PNG whose decoded pixels match the previous baseline (`scripts/png-identical`), so encoder-only churn does not reach the tree.

- **#45 Spreadsheet geometry** — the width/scale/range rules landed 2026-08-14 (code comments on `SheetGridBuilder.MaxDigitWidth` / `ToPoints` and `SpreadsheetParser.ResolveRange` / `ResolveScale`, pinned by `ColumnWidthTests`). Still open:
  - **`weekly-lesson-planner`'s rows stand about 4% short of Excel's** since its print area keeps its empty column A (2026-11-01): its grid is 611px wide against Excel's 610, from 632, and the too-large scale had been hiding rows that Excel draws taller than Morph does. The same body-font factor as below; it scores 0.08 SSIM worse for it.
  - **The factor, measured 2026-11-01** (`_probe_rowfont_*`: the pink-blue timesheet's package, 20 rows declared 30pt with `customHeight`, gridlines printed at 100% on A4, the body font set per probe), as Excel's printed pitch over the 40px declared: Arial 8 1.000, 9 0.976, 10 0.936, 11 0.988, 12 0.984, 14 0.984, 16 0.944; Calibri 8 1.056, 10 0.948, 11 1.008, 12 1.000, 14 1.000; Constantia 11 1.008, Georgia 11 0.980, Segoe UI 11 0.984, Times New Roman 12 0.968, Verdana 11 1.060. It saw-tooths with size, so it is a rounding, and the printed rows land on whole 600-dpi printer pixels. Three readings of it were tried and do not fit: a ratio of the face's line height to its pixel-rounded line, of the screen's whole-pixel digit width to the printed one, and of the printed default row to a whole-pixel screen default (`_probe_rowauto_*`, the same probes with auto-height rows: Arial 10 prints 16.45px, Calibri 11 19.35, Verdana 11 19.5). Constantia's column unit was checked by the same probes: 8.000px against the 8.014 Morph uses.
  - **Row heights render at a BODY-FONT-dependent factor, and Arial-bodied corpus books now visibly pay it** (isolated 2026-08-14, `_probe_fontgraft`; victim confirmed by triage the same day). The same declared heights draw at ~15/16 under an Arial body and essentially exactly under a Verdana-11 theme. `social-media-editorial-theme-calendar` — Arial 12 body, all rows declared `ht` with `customHeight` — had body row pitch 260px against Excel's 259 BEFORE the landing and 267-268 after: the old too-small fit scale was cancelling the ~6% row-height excess, and the corrected scale exposed it (black header band 168 exact → 170, everything below drifting down cumulatively). Fixing it means applying the per-font factor to declared heights for the faces that carry it, which needs the factor measured per face on real-workbook-derived fixtures — do not model a constant. Likely mechanism (unverified): Excel's print scale derived from the Normal font's GDI metrics on screen vs printer. `household-organizer` measuring ~1.05 against the graft's 1.00 remains the one unexplained residue — plausibly non-`customHeight` rows re-auto-sized at open.
  - **Table rules land off Excel's where rows run short.** Since table styles are drawn (2026-11-01, `TableStyles`, every corpus table) SSIM fell across 22 workbooks while the error metric improved: the rules now drawn sit a pixel or more off Excel's where the rows above them already ran short (simple-list's rows are 15px to Excel's 16), which a blank cell had scored better against. The row pitch is the cause, not the styles.
  - **`autoRowHeightFactor = 1.31` is CONFIRMED** (a probe round claimed it wrong and was retracted — the rendered heights it compared against carry the per-font factor above; dividing it out reproduces 1.31 exactly). Do not revisit without dividing out the body-font factor first.
  - **Probe hygiene, learned twice.** (1) Verify the printer paper by rendering a fixture and checking for a 1123x794 page — Excel REPORTS the A4 it was asked for while a Letter driver silently exports Letter (~8% squeeze), and `Get-PrintConfiguration` reported Letter here while Excel exported A4. (2) Validate any hand-built fixture against a REAL workbook with its fit scale neutralised before trusting its numbers — every fixture cloned from the same minimal base carried an unrepresentative body font, which produced one refuted rule and one wrongly-retracted constant.
  - **Neither metric is trustworthy alone on this subsystem.** Placement fixes scored negative while visibly correct — `home-contents-inventory-list` read −0.09 SSIM on the landing that took its gridline error against Excel's regular 104px pitch from a mean 0.9px to 0.4px (no defect; do not chase) — and `weekly-lesson-planner` scored +0.12 on a change that made its geometry measurably worse. Read extents and crops, per `docs/fidelity-audit.md`.

- **#8 Picture effects.** The colour transforms (duotone/greyscale/washout through one shared `ImageRecolor.Rows` recipe) and the `a:alphaModFix` transparency on shape fills landed 2026-08-29 on all four outputs (`docs/word-features.md`). Still open, none of it in the corpus: shape fills carry no COLOUR transforms (`FloatingShapeElement` has no effect fields); `a:clrChange` (Set Transparent Color) and `a:biLevel` parse to `None`, and are per-pixel rules the colour-matrix recipe cannot carry; soft-focus/blur is unmodelled. Office 2010's picture corrections (`a14:brightnessContrast`, `saturation`, `colorTemperature`, in business/04, labels/08, letters/03 and newsletters/03/09) need nothing: Word stores the corrected picture as the blip and the original as an HD Photo layer, and newsletters/07, once listed here for its warm tone, carries no effect at all and matches Word.
- **#47 Charts** go through the sheet chart composer since 2026-10-21 (`docs/word-features.md`, Charts). Still open: 3-D charts with `c:rAngAx` 0 are drawn obliquely where PowerPoint draws them in perspective (`chart-3d-pie`'s 3-D line, `chart-3d-area`, `chart-surface`), and a 3-D line's ribbon and a 3-D area's slab depth are not measured; PowerPoint shades a 3-D surface's bands by their slope, and its contour plot overlays shading, where they are drawn flat; a 3-D bubble is shaded by a linear gradient where PowerPoint lights a sphere; data labels on 3-D and surface charts are not drawn; pie of pie and bar of pie draw as a plain pie; a chart with a secondary axis beside a right legend stands its axis labels about 5px left of PowerPoint's since the side legend's gap landed (chart-combo, 2026-11-01); and the HTML export draws only a pie or doughnut's slices.
- **#13 Footnotes/endnotes** — page-bottom pinning, the separator rule and document-end endnotes landed 2026-09-05 (`Fragmenter.CommitFootnotes` / `PlaceEndnotes`, `FootnoteLayoutTests`; the laws and the fourteen `_probe_fn_*` readouts are in `docs/word-features.md`, Footnotes).
